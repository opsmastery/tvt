## Must have (in this order)

1. **Cost per signal** (`pkg/cost`). Load the price card from `tvt.yaml` (format v1 in `docs/pricecard.md`: strict decoding, required `version`, included allotments applied to the total, vendor presets marked approximate). Metrics: active series × scrape interval from cardinality. Labels: the share of a metric's series a label multiplies. Every signal gets $/month and the observation time. On Grafana Cloud, series already aggregated by an Adaptive Metrics rule are marked "already handled" and not counted as savings.
2. **Loki bytes per stream.** Bytes per day for each stream from Loki's volume API (`/loki/api/v1/index/volume`, fixture `testdata/lab/loki/volume_by_stream.json`), aggregated to the stream signals Sprint 2 writes, and per-label volume so a label drop on a stream can be priced. Every request sends `X-Query-Tags: Source=tvt`.
3. **Scoring and confidence** (`pkg/score`). Usage weight from Sprint 2's evidence classes: declared and incident evidence never decays; observed evidence uses the 15-day half-life, with last-seen dates kept indefinitely. Confidence from the observation window (nothing is recommended from less than `window_days`, default 30) and each source's coverage. Finish the dormant class: signals that runbooks or postmortems only mention (Sprint 2 item 7 collects the names) join the name and tier rules, and dormant signals show as "dormant: confirm", never as drop candidates.
4. **Recommendation records and `tvt report`** (`pkg/report`). The JSON report is the internal API that the HTML report and Sprint 4's `propose` read. Each recommendation (drop, downsample, reroute, label removal, and each rule finding) carries: `claim` (one testable sentence), `evidence` (refs, query-log lines, gaps), `cheapest_check` (the fastest way to confirm or refute it, from ownership, runbook and dormant data), `check_minutes`, `downstream` (every reader with its evidence class), `savings`, `risk_if_wrong` (evidence class, dormant flag, tier tag), `assumptions` (gaps, assumed refs), `owner`, and `status` (open, confirmed, dismissed with a reason). Rank by savings per minute of checking. HTML from an embedded template; if time runs short, ship JSON plus Markdown and cut the polished HTML.
5. **Evidence gaps.** The report header lists coverage per source and every gap (unconfigured source, unproven query log, unreachable runbook, Cloud-only blind spots, assumed refs). `tvt.yaml` acknowledges a gap with a reason; an unacknowledged gap is shown on every affected row, and Sprint 4's `check` fails on it.
6. **Pipeline targets** (ADR 0007). Read `pipelines:` from `tvt.yaml` and match each signal against every pipeline's `handles:` selectors. Exactly one match is its target; none or two is "unmapped", reported with its savings but not proposable, and recorded as a gap. Golden test against the `pipelines:` section of the lab ground truth.
7. **Label-drop safety.** Only recommend dropping a label when the remaining labels still identify every series uniquely: compare series counts with and without the label (Prometheus series API, or Mimir's cardinality API) and refuse the drop if series would merge. Use Sprint 2 item 13's "always aggregated out" list as the candidate source. Label drops stay behind a flag until a partner's precision review passes.

## Should have

8. **Cost per rule and rule-efficiency findings** (report only; tvt never edits rules). Cost per rule for Prometheus, Mimir and Loki rulers and Grafana-managed rules: evaluations per day × samples (Prometheus query log) or bytes processed (Loki `metrics.go`) per evaluation, joined to the rule as Sprint 2 item 12's spike found. Rank rules by daily cost beside signals; on Grafana Cloud, findings ship without a dollar figure. Findings, each with its fix: Grafana-managed alert on Loki or Prometheus (move it to the datasource ruler or a recording rule); LogQL alert that parses raw logs every interval (Loki recording rule); range window far longer than the interval; broad stream selector or no line filter before a parser; interval shorter than the scrape or ingest interval; duplicate rules; recording rule nothing reads; rule with no series for 30 days (broken, not unused); rule group slower than its interval. Golden test against `rule_findings:` in the ground truth.
9. **Loki query-log liveness probe.** Like Sprint 2's proven Prometheus query log: a marker query that selects no streams, sent with `X-Query-Tags: Source=tvt-probe`, must appear in Loki's `metrics.go` lines, otherwise Loki query coverage is 0 and recorded as a gap.
10. **Line-level hand-off to zeroreads** (ADR 0006). For the top log streams, the dominant unread pattern from Loki's patterns API (`/loki/api/v1/patterns`) with its share of bytes and estimated saving, pointing to zeroreads for the proven line-level cut. tvt still ranks and acts on whole streams.
11. **Scale test.** Synthetic cardinality (Prometheus `avalanche`) at a few million series: record scan time and peak memory in `docs/architecture.md`, and add a benchmark that fails on a large regression.
